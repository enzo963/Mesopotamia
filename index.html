AFTERTHE FLOOD

Embark on a unique 3D journey through time. Explore meticuously reconstructed historical environments and immerse yourself in the architecture, culture, and vibrant atmosphere of ancient Mesopotamia. No combat, no pressures—just pure exploration, meaningful discovery, and history brought to life like never before.

Begin your voyage in Babylon, recreated as a living, immersive world waiting to be explored. This is just the start of our journey. Future updates will introduce the wonders of the Assyrian, Sumerian, and Akkad civilizations, expanding this experience into a definitive digital archive of human history.

The World of Ancient Civilizations

Step into the heart of the mighty Assyrian Empire. Witness the grandeur of imperial Palaces, marvel at the detailed stone reliefs, and experience the sheer scale of a civilization known for its architectural ambition and powerful presence in the ancient near east.

Wander through the legendary streets of Babylon. Walk the Processional Way to the magnificent Ishtar Gate, and experience the splendor of a city that was a beacon of law, astronomy, and architectural marvels.

Explore the cradle of urban life in Sumer. Discover the first known city-states, walk beneath massive Ziggurat temples, and connect with the innovations of the people who developed writing and laid the very foundation of organized civilization.

The Application

Complement your experience with our dedicated application. View high-fidelity 3D models of Mesopotamian artifacts, access in-depth historical guides, and dive deeper into the context of the world you explore in the game. It's the ultimate toolkit for history enthusiasts on the go.

Meet Enki, your digital guide to the ancient world. Leveraging advanced technology, Enki provides real-time historical context, translates ancient inscriptions, and shares the forgotten stories of the civilizations you discover, ensuring you don't just see history, but understand it.
